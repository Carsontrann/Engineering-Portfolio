Manufacturing & Machining Experience

Working directly with manufacturing operators gave me a stronger understanding of how CAD models and engineering drawings are translated into physical aerospace components. I gained exposure to machining strategy, programming, workholding, fixture design, and production planning.

5-Axis CNC Machining

I had one-on-one sessions with experienced 5-axis machine operators to better understand how complex aerospace components are programmed, set up, and machined. I gained insight into tool access, machining sequences, part orientation, workholding, and the planning required before a part reaches the machine.

CNC Lathe Operations

I also worked one-on-one with lathe operators to learn how turning operations are planned and programmed. These sessions helped me understand tool selection, machining order, part setup, workholding, and how rotational components are produced from raw material.

Fixture Design & Manufacturing Support

Through one-on-one discussions with 5-axis and lathe operators, I learned how machinists and programmers approach part orientation, tool access, clamping, machining sequences, and workholding. I applied these insights when designing manufacturing fixtures in SolidWorks, considering how components would be located, supported, secured, and accessed during machining.

Additional Manufacturing Processes
